On 15 June 2026 at 23:47 UTC, Colonel Sarah Mitchell, a British officer assigned to the NATO Rapid Response Command, directed elements of the 2nd Mechanized Battalion during Operation Silent Horizon after intelligence reports indicated suspicious activity near Forward Operating Base Sentinel located in the Al-Qamar region at coordinates 31.7683°N, 35.2137°E. The resulting event triggered an emergency coordination meeting between NATO Rapid Response Command, the Joint Intelligence Directorate, and the 5th Armored Brigade, leading to the deployment of additional Leopard 2A7 vehicles and counter-drone equipment.
